## Gotchas and decisions

- Decision by the user: "do not kill container or impact my running applications inside". The memory note records it as a standing rule for any diagnosis of claude-dev.
- The symptom looks like a broken container, and the host looks idle. Host load average and `free` do not show the problem.
- The slice files show it: `cpu.stat` (nr_throttled) and `cpu.pressure` under /sys/fs/cgroup/user.slice/user-644434775.slice.
- The same files of the other user slices are world-readable. Comparing them separates "my limit" from "the host is busy".
- Programs in the container see 32 CPUs. `nproc` printed 32 there three seconds before the batch started.
- The container has no limit of its own. It shares the 8 CPUs, the 64 GiB RAM and the 8 GiB swap with every host login of the user. The rule also sets TasksMax=8192.
- Container files are readable from the host through /proc/1572206/root, session scratchpads included. No `podman exec` is needed.
- `oomctl` works without sudo. `dmesg` and the journal do not.
- systemd-oomd kills every process of one cgroup when the slice stalls on memory above 50% for 20 s. The whole container is one cgroup.
- `pgrep -u jhan -f "python3 -u - 1500"` reported two leftover sampler processes. That was a false match. The processes 3953607 and 3953608 belonged to the user's Codex wait loop and must not be signalled.
- Never signal processes by name in or around the container. A `pkill -x sleep` on 2026-09-07 stopped the container. The memory note claude-box-keepalive-pkill-hazard records that incident.
- One container shell costs one inotify instance and no kernel key. So the session keyrings come from ssh logins, not from container shells.
- The chat answer of 19:05 cited 274 samples up to 19:01:35. It read those while the sampler still ran. The report uses the final numbers: 278 samples up to 19:01:43, and 209 of 5,664 periods throttled.
- The two stall figures for 18:41 and 20:02 are estimates. They back-calculate the 5-minute average with the factor e^(t/300 s). They assume no stall after the job.
- This session ran in the container on sw-dev-01, not on agentsrv. Its scratchpad is not on the shared home.
