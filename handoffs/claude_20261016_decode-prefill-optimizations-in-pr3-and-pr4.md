## Results

No new test ran. All numbers below are read from the 2026-09-14 trace record and the 2026-09-15 and PR 3 campaign records, as the workflow readers confirmed them. The traced "main" is the row-major build of PR 3879 at 544ca05c7a (2026-09-11), qwen3-4b, 8 users, prompt 1,024 tokens, CPU attention.

Prefill, per layer, main thread, from the trace [exec/results/vnnik-trace-20260914]:

| Arm | Save K | Save V | K + V as share of one prefill pass | Helpers busy during the save | First Pending section starts after Save V ends |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| tp2 | 274 us | 295 us | 5.2 % | no (all idle) | 0.3 us |
| tp4 | 281 us | 295 us | 7.6 % | no (all idle) | 0.3 us |

For scale, the VNNI scatter store that motivated PR 3 was 858 us per layer, about 3 times larger than main's Save K.

How much of the removed store time became TTFT, from the 2026-09-15 striping-only arm [exec/results/vnnik2-20260915]: 63 % at tp2, 107 % at tp4. Scaled to main's K + V save:

| Arm | Est. TTFT gain if the whole save were shared | Baseline TTFT |
|---|---|---|
| tp2 | est. 1.4 to 2.6 % (45 to 80 ms) | 3.18 s |
| tp4 | est. about 6 % (about 130 ms) | 2.21 s |

Decode, per layer, from the trace:

| Arm | Save K + Save V | Share of the decode step | Workers still running Ready sections when Save V ends | Helpers idle per layer |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| tp2 | 10 to 13 us | 2.7 to 4.3 % | yes, by 110 us | 227 us of a 346 us layer (66 %) |
| tp4 | 10 to 13 us | 2.7 to 4.3 % | yes, by 34 us | not quoted |

So nothing in decode waits for the save, and PR 3's gate skips passes of 16 items or fewer. The stack's decode gains are layout-bound: PR 2's kernel with AMX on gives +5.4 % and +9.5 % TPS versus main at tp2, and PR 1's reader with AMX off gives +3.4 % and +4.5 % [pr3-E-cpu/summary.md and the PR bodies, as read at 08:29].

PR 3 line classification (workflow reader): 352 of PR 3's 712 added lines (49 %) are the layout-independent sharing protocol (window struct, unit cut, claim loop and join, worker-index plumbing, code-generator helper call). 277 lines (transpose, store_block, set_k_block) are layout-specific.

PR 4: its FPGA-attention gains against its parent are +2.5 % at tp2 and +12.1 % at tp4, and they return the stack to main (+0.6 % and -0.3 % against main, inside the band). Three of its mechanisms restore a property main already has. Ported alone to main, the only effect would be cost.
