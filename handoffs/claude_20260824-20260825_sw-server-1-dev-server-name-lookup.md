## Gotchas & decisions

  switched to ps -eo args | grep "[/]bin/bash ./sample.sh" for liveness.
- alpha's sampler lost samples during the two NFS stalls, so gaps in
  alpha.csv are the stall windows, not sampler bugs.
- Load average alone misled at first glance: absolute load was similar, but
  sw-dev-01 has half the cores, so per-core busyness differs about 2x even
  when idle.
