## 7. Gotchas & decisions

- **Sampled profiles lied**: LBR stack collapse in inlined spin loops produced a fake "uniform per-layer K/V-wait doubling" — suspicious uniformity was the fingerprint. Wall-clock TSC probes are the ground truth here; treat sampled spin attribution with distrust.
- **Never edit a script a running campaign executes** (bash reads incrementally; killed a campaign once). Always `cp` a per-campaign harness copy.
- 3bda has `perf_event_paranoid=4`: all perf needs sudo (non-root perf stat silently writes header-only files); vtune threading needs `kernel.yama.ptrace_scope=0`.
- The `HW attention disabled` banner lands 15–55 ms after the load-complete log line — poll with `wait_marker`, never instant-grep.
- pfgate trace numbering traps: extreme draw dirs are renamed (`draw_11-slowest`); the SQL window ordinal needs `-1` to match the python pipeline's 0-based `gaps.csv` numbering; window splitter (1.5 ms silence) and stall threshold (span > 1500 µs) are the same number only by coincidence.
- Probe tag map: 0xx Q-wait, 1xxx KV latch, 2xxx barrier, 3xxx tx, 4xxx rx, 7000 fill, 7100 alloc, 7200 prepare, 7400 callback, 7500+(block&255) per-block instants, 7600 Fence 3, 7601 Fence 1, 7900+dev HBM audit. At tp1 the per-block family can reach 7617 — disambiguate by `dur==0` (instants) vs nonzero (fence records).
- **Terminology rules (standing, user-enforced)**: "inter-fwd-pass" (not "boundary path (parent)"), "share of level 1" (not "share of parent"), define corr wherever used, no invented shorthand ("carrier" banned), plain English, "definitive sync point" is adopted project terminology (Fence 1 and Fence 3 are the only two per token cycle; Fence 2 is not one).
- **Reporting rules (standing)**: two-level attribution with reconciliation residuals in every drill-down; regenerate accreting HTML pages from scratch rather than patching (user caught stale contradictions repeatedly); check diagrams for label overlap/cutoff (user sends screenshots); lane diagrams to scale.
- The HTML generator scripts (gen_report.py, gen_3c51_step*.py, etc.) lived in the session scratchpad under /tmp/claude-0/ and are **gone** — regenerating any status page from scratch means rewriting a generator; the pages themselves are the surviving record.
- Attribution shares move with campaign TPS spread — the honest reconciled numbers are 68.3%/71.8% (TSC) and 96.7% (pfgate window view), superseding an early "~98%" phrasing.
- Ungated (full) perfetto tracing drops TPS to 107–129 tok/s and widens the gap to ~20% — analyze such traces for structure, never for comparable numbers.
- vtune threading draws (~126 tok/s) are excluded from TPS distributions (instrumentation overhead).
- 2x tp2 concurrent-instances experiment was proposed and dropped at user pushback (llama already answered it; discrimination muddy).
