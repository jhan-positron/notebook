## Words used here

- **tron**: Positron's inference program (GitHub repo positron-ai/tron). **runtron**: its single-model server binary.
- **AMX**: Intel Advanced Matrix Extensions, the tile matrix unit used by the attention kernel. **AVX loop**: the software attention path on AVX-512 vector instructions.
- **AoF / FPGA attention**: attention on FPGA, the mode where the FPGA card scores the keys it has copied. **GOF**: group of four tokens, the unit the card copies (gof_tokens = 4).
- **Page**: 64 tokens of K and V, the unit the AMX kernel takes. **Ready page**: a full page. **Pending page**: the partly filled last page.
- **Kernel unit**: the number of tokens an AMX kernel call takes at once (64 today).
- **Copy lag**: during prefill, pages whose GOF copy to the card has not finished yet, which the CPU scores instead.
- **Visit**: one apply_page_tok call. **K tokens**: keys scored, counted once per KV head.
- **Exit report**: the per-run attention counter dump that runtron prints at exit (PR 4596 counters, merged to main 2026-10-01).

Under software attention the kernel's decode work is 4,461 ready-page visits plus 3 pending-page visits per unit. Under FPGA attention the card takes all 4,461 ready pages whatever its copy unit, as long as that unit divides 64. The unit mismatch removes only the last 3 visits (0.07 %).

## Gotchas and decisions

- The AMX-off arm's zero AMX visits is forced by the kill switch and proves nothing. The counter that carries the information in that arm is avx_full_page_visits (AMX-eligible whole pages that did not take the AMX path). It is also 0.
- The "divides 64" qualifier is needed. For copy units that do not divide 64 (3, 5, 6, 12, 48, 128) some ready-page keys stay on the CPU.
- The prefix-cache shares (3.0 to 3.6 % and 1.4 %) are against the closed-form cold count (1,258,272 at prompt 1,000, 1,285,632 at prompt 1,024). Against the measured prompt-1,000 control (1,239,648 per request) they are 3.1 to 3.6 %.
- The full-card-store case has no kernel-share counter. It is recorded by the engines' "lose HW attention" warnings, the sw_fallback_total FUSE counter, and the TTFT delta.
- Semicolons in the page sit inside bracketed citation lists, the existing page convention, and were kept.
