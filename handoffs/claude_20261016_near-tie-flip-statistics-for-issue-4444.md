## Short version

### Replay on the real logged Q, K, V (exec/td-20261016/replay/results.md, layer 0, 201 sampled query tokens, 32 heads)

- Q, K and V of layer 0 are identical in all eight arms.
- Dotter against reader: 64 % of the 912,032 raw scores differ, median 1 fp32 ulp. The within-pair order matters: even-first changes 23 % of the reader's scores.
- Order-only pairs change 0.05 % of the bf16 output elements, usually one element by one notch, at about 40 % of the tokens. Rounding pairs change 20 % to 30 % of the elements at every token with a dense page (0 % in prefill pass 1, 19.5 % in pass 2, 28 % in decode).
- Against exact fp64: the truncated-weight paths are biased low by 0.08 % of the output (weight mass loss 0.16 %, up to 0.34 %), RMS relative error 2.0e-3. The round-to-nearest path has no bias (1.6e-5) and RMS 1.6e-3.
- Validation: the replay predicts the first differing tokens 26 (order pairs) and 128 (rounding pairs), which the logged WO records show. The replay output pushed through the o_proj weights matches the logged WO at 1.4e-3 to 2.4e-3 relative L2, the floor that rounding alone produces (1.61e-3 at token 0).
- Layer 1 gives the same picture: dotter against reader 58.5 %, clean bias -0.11 %.

### Near-tie flip statistics (exec/td-20261016/tiestats/tie_stats.md)

Section 1, from the p83-20261013 pairs and a 2026-08-30 record: the margin is a median 3.875 logit units (39 ulps) at prompt 1024 and 7.25 (48.8 ulps) at prompt 2048. The pair movement is a median 0.0625 logit units (0.5 to 1 ulp), p95 0.375 to 0.5 (2 to 3 ulps). The flip model (margin plus movement crosses zero, ties to the lower id) reproduces every observed flip. Its predictive form expects 0.76 to 1.54 flips per 256 steps per pair against 0 to 3 observed, 6.9 expected against 6 observed over 1,536 steps.

Section 2, the ten round-1 pairs: two pairs are bit-identical controls. The other eight show 9 flips in 2,048 steps, all at steps 20, 84 or 248. The model expects 12.38 flips (own movements) or 12.41 (pooled) against 9 observed, with P(count at most 9) = 0.18.

### Verdict (as the report states it)

- Cause 1: three fp32 add orders (dotter, reader, AMX tile). Not a defect. All three are correct summations.
- Cause 2: the AVX-512 PV path truncates the softmax weights, the AMX kernel rounds them to nearest. A pre-existing systematic error of the AVX-512 path: bias -0.08 %, error 1.13 to 1.8 times that of rounding to nearest. No quality regression has been attributed to it.
- Cause 3: at the PR 4959 head only, the page-end decode step scores its just-completed page in the software loop where main uses AMX (the block converts to VNNI only at the forward end). Not a defect, but an asymmetry that prevents a bit-exact match with main. PR 4957 and 4958 behave like main (code reading).
- Which cells can have which cause: the AMX kernels serve head size 128 with 4 query heads per KV head only. qwen-3-30b-a3b has cause 1 only, gpt-oss-120b none, llama-3.1-8b all three.
- Design decisions (fix-design page): D1 kill-switch option C (AVX-512 kernels in the AMX order on dense pages when the switch is on, bit-identical to the AMX binary, about 120 lines of kernels), D2 round to nearest in scaled_v_expr (one function, goldens regenerate), D3 convert the completed block before attention in PR 4959, D4 a model-level forced-run bound test in CI, D5 the numerics contract text.
