- **The map.** Each of the 32 crosspoints with slices on mesh 1 has one proxy crosspoint on mesh 0, one-to-one. Examples: (5,2) of mesh 1 pairs with (2,5) of mesh 0, (1,4) with (5,1), and (5,5) with (1,2). The full map is in the page's figure `proxymap` and in proxyfit/proxy-positions.json.

- **A second test without core positions** compares raw latencies and gives the same 32 proxy crosspoints. In that test the latency of a home node of mesh 1 differs from the latency of the slice at its proxy crosspoint by at most 0.33 ns (RMS over the cores of mesh 0).
- **Bit 6 rule.** The fitted proxy crosspoint of a line equals the measured home crosspoint of the line that differs in PA bit 6 only, for 262,144 of 262,144 pairs.
