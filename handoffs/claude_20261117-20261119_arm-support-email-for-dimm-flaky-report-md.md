## Words used here

- **DIMM, module**: one memory module of 32 GiB. CRB has 12 slots, 6 per half of the chip. `DIMM_CP0_5` is slot 5 of half 0.
- **MT/s**: million transfers per second, the unit of memory speed. CRB ran at 8000 MT/s until 19:43 UTC on 2026-11-17 and at 7200 MT/s after that.

- **Training, "DCS delay training"**: training is the step at power-on where the firmware tunes the signal timing of each module. "DCS delay training" is one training step.

- **NCKK**: the last part of the part number of the CRB modules (Micron `MTC20F2085S1RC80BH1 NCKK`).
- **Error record**: a message that the firmware passes to Linux when hardware reports an error. Linux prints it in the kernel log with the tag `[Hardware Error]`.
- **BMC**: the management controller on the board. **SCP**: the system control processor, which runs the first firmware stage.
- **Arm welcome kit**: Arm's support files for CRB in /home/jhan/workspace/vendor-resources/ARM/agi-cpu-welcome-kit.
