Facts verified by the workflow and recorded in the issue:

- Why it works today: clang 19 assigns every vector load and store the "may overlap anything" TBAA tag (`CodeGenTBAA.cpp`, marked "For now" in the source) and lowers the pointer arithmetic to byte offsets. Verified on standalone programs with the zig-packaged clang 19.1.7 at `-O3 -std=gnu++23`. Not established: that the nix-built clang on delphi-3bda emits the same tags.
- What would detect a break: nothing in the build or test flow. clang has no aliasing warning that fires here, UBSan has no aliasing check, the ASan list excludes `t_llama_unit`, and TySan exists only from LLVM 20.
- The 8-lane build does not compile on main, at the PR 4557 head or at the PR 4587 head: a syntax-only check of `t_llama_unit.cpp` gives 131 errors (58 in `kernels/wide.hpp`, 37 in the test file, none in `kv_cache.hpp`), the same at c73e7fb2f9, 633cb88896 and e6c53ba70a. Whether anyone builds or runs an 8-lane binary is not established. The configure routes are the `darwin` preset, the `native` preset on a host without AVX-512, and `-DAVX512=OFF`.
- The comment said the 8-lane accessors "read" the vector array, but the V save path in `model.hpp` also writes through them. The issue notes this.
