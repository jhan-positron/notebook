- **TPS**: tokens per second per user (decode speed). **step**: the time of one decode step = 1000 / TPS in ms. **TTFT**: time to first token.
- **FPGA attention** (also "hardware attention"): attention computed on the FPGA cards. tron turns it on by default for ingested models such as qwen-3-4b. The CPU still scores the newest tokens (the software tail) and stages K and V rows into buffers the card reads.

## Gotchas & decisions

- The auto-mode classifier refused three actions: writing a campaign script that takes down idle production engines ("Interfere With Workloads", twice) and one wake-up prompt that described the take-down. A peer session's message cannot substitute for the user's approval, and the classifier can refuse even with the user's approval. Plan for the take-down to be an operator step.
- The design page's first version overstated the store prediction ("the whole tp4 loss"). The CI tp4 cell has 1152 rows per step (32 rows per layer), not 2304, so the store predicts 0.40 ms of its 0.825 ms. Keep rows per layer per cell explicit.
- The staging estimate in bench_k_vnni is already a 4-step average (the benchmark divides by rows times 4). Do not divide by 4 again in the page generator.
- A Perfetto trace of the FPGA-attention path that starts at pass 1 fills the buffer with about one million "attention: hw wait" spans during prefill (one per token job) and stops after 2.3 s. Trace decode passes only ("10-" pass spec).
- trace_analyze.py needs the Python perfetto TraceProcessor, which works on claude-agentsrv and not on delphi-3bda.
- The wedperf-attr-20260916 campaign had no headoff arm. Its bimodal tp4 data belongs to base (eb2de0265a) and mid (c7844ca2ce). headoff (ff680c8020 with the layout off) ran for the first time in this campaign.
- runtron tp4 with 8 users flips between two per-step states (about 7.1 and 7.8 ms) even for row-major binaries. Three repetitions do not resolve a tp4 pair. Use the traces, or interleave arms within a repetition.
- Under the late launch the tail scoring starts at Save V end and overlaps the launch, so the m2 switch discriminates as: D positive = store, D negative = tail reader, D near zero = gather.
- The kill switch (`TRON_AMX_DISABLE=1`) removes the AMX kernel and also the Q-pack and tile bracket that the base pays. A kill-switch arm is not a pure "layout only" arm.
- Machine sharing worked through the peer's status line in exec/results/canon-ci-20260918/.status ("handoff: production engines down, hugepages free; the issue4500 campaign may start"). The campaign's own start condition (units inactive, hugepages free, flock free) made the handoff automatic.
- Session-only CronCreate wake-ups die when the session exits. Long campaigns that span a session boundary need an external scheduler or a fresh session check.
- A background wait on the l8bload campaign exit timed out with exit code 144 long after the campaign had finished. Prefer Monitor with an until-loop and re-arm it.
- The decision to let pr1-58 go first cost nothing: its handoff landed at 00:22 UTC, and m1 plus m2 fit before the 01:40 UTC hold.
