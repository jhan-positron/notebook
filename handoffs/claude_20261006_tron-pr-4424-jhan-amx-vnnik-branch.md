## What the change does

One commit, four files, +92/-92 lines [commit f52d634c32 on branch jhan-amx-vnniK-savek-worker].

- `save_k(q_batch, ks, worker_ix = 0, n_workers = 1)` on both overloads. Worker 0 cuts the work units, opens the K store window, stores its share, joins the helpers and marks the tokens. Workers 1 and up wait for their window, store their share and leave. `save_k_helper` is deleted.
- The generator emits `save_k(..., 0, n_workers)` in `run()` and `save_k(..., worker_ix, n_workers)` in `run_main_help`, at the same statement position. The literal 0 is the one the generator already uses for main-side kernel calls.
- The generator spec expects two `save_k` calls per KV-writing operation.
- The three-thread unit test calls `save_k` on every thread. It now also checks that each token's K is marked exactly once. A helper that ran main's mark loop would fail that check.
- Handwritten plugins are untouched. They take the defaults (worker 0 of 1) and store every row on the calling thread.
