## Gotchas & decisions

- platformd v0.10.7 uses the legacy posadm provisioning path. A same-model provision never restarts engines, so after every package switch the engines must be restarted (dut.sh serving-down, then serving-up) or they keep running the deleted old binary.
- The rinzler journal has no per-request line. Per-engine request counts come from the FUSE stats file delphi-3bda:/var/run/rinzler/N/rinzler/stats/prompts_total (readable as jhan, no sudo). The idle test in dut.sh is decided by the `#EVT# SYSTEM_STATS` line, which appears every 5 to 6 minutes.
- ShareGPT conversation lengths with the llama-3.1-8b tokenizer: min 1511, median 2045, max 21126 tokens; 501 of 1000 are shorter than 2048. Prompts above that need the concatenation change in testlib/prompt.py. The prompt-1024 output is unchanged by it.
- The server truncates llama-3.1-8b-instruct-good prompts to 4096 tokens and llama-3.1-70b-instruct-good prompts to 8192, in CI and production alike, because the weights store holds the 2024-08-13 upstream revision of tokenizer.json. rinzler reports the truncated count as the prompt size and raises no error. A long-prompt cell on these models measures a shorter context than it claims.
- Check-cell verdicts: only rc 124 with 'Running round' in driver.log, or rc 2 with FAILED_CONFIGS naming the check config, are evidence about the prompt length. rc 1 is a harness crash and rc 7 the stale-binary stop. The first campaign.sh dropped cells on any non-zero rc (review findings C5 and C6 in review-findings.txt).
- The restore path must wait for the DUT and retry ssh-level failures (rc 255 or 124 with empty output) but not dut.sh's own failures. The outage that stops a campaign would otherwise also defeat its restore and leave the canon deb installed (review finding D1 in review2-findings.txt).
- Bash: `if run_pass; then ...; fi; rc=$?` gives the if-statement's status, never the command's. Capture rc first. A retry helper that prints with `say` to stdout pollutes `$(helper | awk ...)` captures; send messages to stderr and validate captured values (a sha must match `^[0-9a-f]{64}$`).
- The harness prints 'anomalous tps=' lines in its worker processes. Count anomalous samples from the Talos stub's samples in the parent. After a failed cell the ProcessPool workers block forever; exit with os._exit after killing multiprocessing.active_children().
- Peer coordination: another session's campaign may hold the campaign flock only during each runtron run. Gate on `pgrep -u jhan -f '[r]untron[. ]|[i]ssue4500.*campaign[.]sh'` (a pattern that does not match itself), not on `flock -n`.
- The Bash tool blocks a `sleep 120` chained with other commands (record at 07:26 PDT). Use the Monitor tool with an until-loop, or run the command in the background.
