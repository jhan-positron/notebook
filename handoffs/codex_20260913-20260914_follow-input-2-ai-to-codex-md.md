## Current state

The benchmark audit found decode gains in five tested configurations and worse first-token latency in three. TTFT means time to first token. TPS means tokens per second per user. TP2 and TP4 mean two or four accelerator cards per model engine. The one-engine campaign used eight users on one engine, while nightly testing used eight users across eight cards. Nightly TPS was an average per-user rate, not a sum across engines. [Transcript: lines 526, 1033, 1068, 1136]

The review kept the candidate experimental. It identified missing feature-enabled continuous integration, the reader’s 16-query-head limit, and an unresolved generation divergence at zero-based token index 52. The same-binary controls reproduced all 128 output tokens. That narrows the numerical question but does not prove a harmless near-tie. [codex-review-claude.html; codex-perf-audit.json]

The proposed block writer replaces 64 vector scatters with 64 contiguous vector stores for 16 tokens of one 128-dimension key head. Both write 4,096 bytes. “16 times fewer store instructions” was corrected. Coordinated helper saving needs ownership and completion rules to avoid deadlock and early publication. Neither remedy was proven by this chat. [Transcript: line 1417]
