## Gotchas and decisions

- A set of FUSE leaves copied one file at a time is not one consistent read. The 2026-09-29 snapshot took 0.49 s for 131 files, and the run advanced about 19 decode forwards meanwhile. Take intervals from the same leaf, never differences across leaves.
- At bff317e0d3 (sample A's binary) the listener count was added at the start of a forward and the token count at its end. A live read showed listener_jobs 8 ahead of token_jobs mid-forward. Commit 04da001cb5 moved the three job counts together. The page explains this in section 7.
- T2 (attn_elapsed) excludes the upstream K and V wait at the described head. The figure 1 text of respond-Wade-comments.html says otherwise and is wrong for this head.
- A prefill chunk's own pages always take the AVX path. A fresh page has one visible range per token, so the one-visible-range clause of is_dense_amx_page fails. The page cites the comment in self_attention.hpp.
