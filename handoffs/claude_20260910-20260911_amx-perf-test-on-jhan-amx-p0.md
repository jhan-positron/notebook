## Gotchas & decisions

- CI-busy detection on delphi-3bda: busy only if /run/lock/systems-test-ci.lease exists AND state == "busy" AND now < expires_at_epoch. launch.sh requires three consecutive clear polls a minute apart before starting. The last five nightlies released the machine between 11:18Z and 11:29Z.
- pgrep self-match trap (bit at 11:32Z): `others=$(pgrep -f PATTERN | grep -vx "$$")` inside a script finds the $(...) subshell, which has the same command line. Exclude $BASHPID as well. The campaign exited on its own from this and had to be relaunched.
- Abort path: a plain `kill <pid>` of the detached campaign runs the EXIT trap but does not kill the foreground runtron or the CI harness, and would leave jhan-owned /dev/hugepages/slice-4..7-of-8 files. The reviewed fix makes finish() kill the child process group and remove our slice files. Check /dev/hugepages after any manual abort.
- The campaign stops idle rinzler serving before its runs ("standing policy, round 1 recipe") and frees all 512 hugepages. It checks the journal for request lines first and only stops units that are idle.
- "CI setup" here means the nightly's throughput harness (systems_test scripts/perf.py) driven against one engine on our half with CPU attention and a stubbed database, not the whole nightly. Its numbers cannot be compared with the Slack "@8u per machine" line: the nightly spreads 8 users over 4 tp2 engines (2 users per engine) with FPGA attention. Per-user TPS falls steeply with users per engine (measured 2026-09-08 on one tp4 engine, AMX-on, CPU attention: 1 user 189, 2 users 159, 4 users 122, 8 users 78.5 TPS; exec/results/g1-20260908/).
- The kill-switch cost claim from earlier pages does not hold on this branch: the 2026-09-09 clean-build arm was not faster than the kill-switch arm, so AMX-off with TRON_AMX_DISABLE=1 is a fair baseline.
- Excluding early-stop runtron runs from the mean is a decision made in this session (summarize.py); they remain in the table, marked EARLY-STOP.
- Two invocations of campaign.sh wrote to the same log; a report generator must take the earliest "campaign started" and the last "campaign finished" line, not the last pair (verification finding C1).
