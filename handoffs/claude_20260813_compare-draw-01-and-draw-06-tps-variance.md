### Section 7.2.2 — where the draw_01 vs draw_06 difference lives

draw_06 = campaign fastest (173.293 tok/s); draw_01 = rank 15/16
(167.205 tok/s); measured F3→F3 period difference +567.6k cycles/token
(TPS-derived 567.3k, 0.05% apart). Tiling with zero residual (means):

| region | draw_06 (cycles) | draw_01 (cycles) | Δ | share of period Δ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| open→F1 (70-worker join wait) | 480k | 873k | +393k (146 µs) | +69.2% |
| forward pass | 13,200k | 13,514k | +314k | +55.3% |
| F1→F3 | 1,465k | 1,372k | −93k | −16.3% |
| F3→end construction | 436k | 390k | −47k | −8.2% |

Inside the +393k join-wait delta, the scheduler's blocking
`workers_done.wait()` carries +306k. Texture: the [open→F1] growth is broad
and tail-heavy (36.4% of draw_01 tokens spend >1,000k cycles there vs 16.6%
in draw_06; median +171k vs mean +393k) and front-loaded (draw_01 quarter
medians decay 525k→340k; draw_06 flat ~245k) — the opposite shape of the
7.2.1 [F1→F3] drift. draw_01 has the campaign's smallest drift amplitude
(+8.7k, verified strict minimum across all 16 dumps) yet is second-slowest,
so drift amplitude and the join-wait cost are separate per-launch lottery
expressions.

### Section 7.2.4 — F1-window traffic check (all 16 draws)

- Finding 1 — wcls (lm-head) is never launched before Fence 1: campaign-wide
  18 of 65,520 tokens have any `tx_launch` within 50k cycles of F1
  (draw_01: 0 of 4,104; minimum gap 38k–57k). The wcls dispatch cut is valid;
  draw_01's cheaper post-F1 legs are not an early-launch artifact.
- Finding 2 — the [open→F1] window is not hardware-quiet: every token has
  ~13–14 trailing dispatches and ~13 rx completions inside it. Window
  anatomy = variable transfer phase + near-constant quiet interval:

  | median, cycles | draw_06 | draw_01 | Δ |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | window [open→F1] | 245.6k | 418.7k | +173k |
  | transfer phase [open→last rx] | 163.6k | 349.8k | +186k |
  | quiet interval [last rx→F1] | 85.5k | 92.1k | +7k |

  Across all 16 draws: transfer-phase median spans 164k–347k,
  corr(transfer median, window median) = 0.93, corr(transfer, TPS) = −0.64;
  the quiet interval sits at 72k–92k (27–34 µs) everywhere
  (corr with TPS −0.23). draw_01's front-loaded decay is entirely in the
  transfer phase (Q1 471k → Q4 315k; quiet flat 86–94k), uniform across all
  4 devices (per-device last-rx medians 251k–334k vs draw_06's 146k–152k).
- Finding 3 — trailing rx completions cross F1 on 93.0% of draw_01 tokens
  (8,359 events; median 2 per crossing token) and 98.7% of draw_06 tokens
  (13,439 events; median 4). The split across F1 is run-dependent: the
  fast-join draw leaves more trailing cleanup executing after F1. This
  confirms the owner's "fences leak work" hypothesis for device traffic
  (instant stamps only, µs-scale); the "state ready pre-F1" hypothesis is
  excluded for wcls; the supported variant is that F1 fires a nearly fixed
  72k–92k cycles after the last trailing rx, so the [open→F1] variance IS the
  transfer-phase variance.
