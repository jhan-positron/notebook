## Gotchas & decisions

- "Page" is overloaded in names. In this work, `book.pages[]` and
  `GAL.pages[4]` are intentionally described with different mental names:
  64-position page views versus source-position accessors.
- GOF/HBM is a one-way staging path for K/V bytes. Attention results come back
  separately and do not update book K/V.
- V staging is swizzled, so visual cells represent capacity contributions rather
  than contiguous byte slices.
