## Gotchas & decisions

- All animation durations are invented teaching values. They are neither measurements nor performance estimates. The page therefore has no measurement-data lineage to backfill.
- Page boundaries do not determine visibility. Excluding a token from a window does not itself delete its cached K/V.
- Attention on ready pages can begin after the query vector is available. Attention on pending pages waits for the required new K/V. The page's timing diagram deliberately simplifies that overlap.
- The first sandboxed browser attempt stalled. A later run outside the sandbox produced the recorded checks. The transcript's process cleanup found that the earlier process had already exited. [Transcript lines 118–208.]
- The validator uses absolute local paths and the temporary browser installation. Inspect those dependencies before rerunning it on another host.
