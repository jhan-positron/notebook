## Gotchas & decisions

- Red-carpet couture can occasionally be sheer or revealing even on mainstream
  sites; movie galleries are only as office-safe as the selected film.
- Getty browsing is free but images are watermarked. Some Vogue and SCMP pages
  may show subscription prompts or paywalls.
- Celebrity-scraping sites such as CelebMafia and ThePlace were deliberately
  excluded because they commonly mix ordinary galleries with bikinis,
  lingerie, or risky advertising.
