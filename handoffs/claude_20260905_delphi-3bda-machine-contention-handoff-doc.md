## What people-check.sh does (from the handoff)

It runs on delphi-3bda, stops at the first failing check, and prints one line.

| # | Check | Verdict | Exit code |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | CI lease busy | WAIT | 1 |
| 2 | Blackout window active | WAIT | 1 |
| 3 | Claim file from another user younger than 12 h | WAIT | 1 |
| 4 | Bill logged in during the last 15 min | YIELD (release our claim); HOLD if our own claim is older than 15 min | 2 (HOLD: 0) |
| 5 | Bill typed in a terminal in the last 30 min (`who -u` idle column) | WAIT | 1 |
| 6 | Bill's processes used over 2 CPU-seconds in a 10 s window, or run tron or build binaries (`other_user_active`) | WAIT | 1 |
| - | none of the above | FREE; `--claim "note"` writes `/var/tmp/3bda-claim.jhan` | 0 |

Thresholds are environment variables: `SAME_TIME_MIN` (15), `KEYBOARD_IDLE_MIN` (30), `CLAIM_MAX_H` (12), `PEOPLE_OTHER` (bill). `--release` removes our claim. The 30-minute retry is a Claude Code `/loop 30m` line or `until ssh delphi-3bda '.../people-check.sh --claim "..."'; do sleep 1800; done`.
