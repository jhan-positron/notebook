Distribution of q over all repetitions with many threads

q is the rate of the thread at the 10th percentile divided by the rate of the thread at the 90th percentile, in one repetition. The provisional rule of the handoff calls a repetition split if q is below 0.65, uniform if q is above 0.85, and between otherwise [Ref2]. The handoff asks to look at the distribution first. If q does not fall into two groups, the labels are not used [Ref2].

One dot is one repetition. Dots are moved a little up or down so that equal values stay visible. The grey band is the range that the rule calls "between". Each row is labelled with its lowest and highest q.

Observed in screening: the 152 values of q do not fall into two groups by the test below. The values lie from 0.53 to 0.99. The rule alone would give 53 split, 34 between and 65 uniform. The largest distance between two neighbouring q values is 0.060 (from 0.611 to 0.671). The test asks for at least 0.10. The values above the distance span 0.67 to 0.99, which is wider than the distance. The values below the distance span 0.53 to 0.61, which is wider than the distance. So this page does not use the labels split and uniform. It gives q itself.

Where the values lie, observed in screening:

  • The 53 values below 0.65 are: 50 of old read at 64+56 threads, 2 of old read at 64+52 threads, 1 of old write at 64+56 threads.
  • For the read kernels at 64+56 threads alone: q is 0.533 to 0.605 for old read (50 repetitions of all steps). It is 0.855 to 0.972 for new read and asm read (26 repetitions). The two ranges do not overlap: no q of these kernels at this count lies between 0.605 and 0.855.
  • The 34 values from 0.65 to 0.85 come from: old read in A4 (12), asm read in A4 (7), old write in A1 (6), old write in A2 (4), asm write in A2 (3), new write in A1 (1), asm write in A1 (1).
  • The handoff asks for the test over all repetitions of Part A [Ref2]. So the labels stay unused for every step and kernel on this page.

The handoff gives no test for "two groups". The analysis script uses this one (choice C2) [Ref5]:

  • The q values are sorted. The largest distance between two neighbours divides them into a lower and an upper group.
  • The values count as two groups if three conditions hold. Each group has at least 2 values. The distance is at least 0.10. The distance is larger than the width of each group.
  • The labels are used only if, in addition, the lower group is below 0.65 and the upper group is above 0.85.

36 repetitions are not in this chart and not in the test. They are the write and copy repetitions of the A3 processes with two buffers. The handoff says to compare only the read rows of A3 [Ref2]. They are listed in the file partA-summary.json under the key q.rows_left_out [Ref4].
