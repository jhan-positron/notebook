<p>The cross-chiplet measurement behind rows 3 and 4, at 16 MiB. A chain is built and walked once on one core, so every line sits in that core's chiplet. A core on the same or the other chiplet then walks two fresh chains over the same lines, one after the other. The first walk from the other chiplet pays the trip across; the second walk finds the lines in the reader's own-mesh slices.</p>
