<p>The cross-chiplet measurement behind rows 3 and 4, at 16 MiB. A chain is built and walked once on one core. After that walk the lines are expected to be in that core's chiplet. A core on the same or the other chiplet then walks two fresh chains over the same lines, one after the other. The first walk from the other chiplet is 56 to 59 ns slower for each load. The second walk is close to a walk on one chiplet. The run did not count where the lines are before each walk. So the slice columns of rows 3 and 4 give the expected state.</p>
