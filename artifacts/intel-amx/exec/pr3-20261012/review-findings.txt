Readers of already-complete tokens read other lanes only; a full block holds only this pass's unmarked tokens, so no reader touches its lines. Both arguments assume no two items of a pass map to the same (page, offset): unverified; gather_token_job_ids (model.hpp 1874-1879) builds a token-to-job map that assumes it too.
