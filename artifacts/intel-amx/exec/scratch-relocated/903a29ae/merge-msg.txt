Merge PR #4557 (typed KV-cache tensors) into the VNNI K branch

The parent branch jhan-kv-typed-tensors (c12df586b6) gives the KV cache
typed owners and views: kv_block::k is one bf16 array with k_view(), V is a
v_vnni_tensor reached through v_packed / set_v / get_v with typed rows, and
the AMX kernels take typed views. This merge keeps every VNNI K change of
PR #4424 (30c4ac82cb) on top of it. Conflicts were resolved as follows.

- kv_cache.hpp: the VNNI K accessors (k_vnni_plane, set_k_row, set_k_block,
  get_k_row) stay before the parent's set_v / get_v / v_packed declarations.
  The parent removed page::v_data, so the old comment that introduced it is
  gone.
- self_attention.hpp: the dense AMX page keeps the layout branch. The
  row-major branch passes the whole K plane as a const_native_k_view, as the
  parent does.
- amx_attn.cpp: both include sets; pack_q_rows_128x4 and qk_vnni_128x4 stay
  next to the parent's typed weights_times_v_128x4.
- t_amx_dispatch_dtype.cpp: the fixture fills K with set_k_row and V with
  v_source_row; the fakes of both PRs coexist.
- t_llama_unit.cpp: the uniform-access concept checks get_k_row and the typed
  get_v; both PRs' new test cases are kept. The sliding-chunk test from main
  reads and writes K through set_k_row / get_k_row, which serve both layouts.
- t_amx_numerics.cpp: the VNNI identity case wraps its K fixture in
  const_native_k_view for qk_rowmajor_128x4.
- common.hpp: both include sets.
- config/test-benchmarks.json: the parent's rows, plus the t_k_vnni_layout
  row. The t_llama_unit row keeps the parent's timing and needs a
  remeasurement (bin/slice bench --update) because the merged binary has
  more cases.

The VNNI plane is still reached through raw bf16 pointers here. The typed
K owner and views (the child step of the issue #4525 design) follow in
later commits.

Verified on claude-box (clang 19, preset native, AMX dispatch on, fake
device): t_k_vnni_layout, t_amx_dispatch_dtype, t_amx_numerics,
t_heterogeneous_scheduler and t_llama_unit pass with TRON_K_VNNI on
(t_llama_unit 45 cases, 246814 assertions) and off (246871 assertions).
