Declined for the alignment literals, per the C++ guide this repository's PRs follow: numeric literals are allowed in `alignas(...)` because the syntax already says the value is an alignment in bytes, and `alignas(64)` is preferred over a constant that only restates it (a named constant is for a shared requirement whose uses must change together). The three sites (`gof.cpp` k_scratch, `model.hpp` conv, and the owner in `h/tron/tensor/k_vnni.hpp`) each state their own alignment and have no shared-requirement coupling.

Also declined for the transpose stage factors in `transpose_16x16_epi32`. `2 * i`, `4 * i`, `i + 4`, `i + 8`, `i + 12`, `LANES_16 / 2` and `LANES_16 / 4` are the lane indices of the four butterfly stages of a 16 x 16 transpose (pairs, then quads, then 128-bit quarters, then halves). They are index arithmetic, not fixed values with a domain meaning of their own, and the function comment states the structure ("Four stages of two-register shuffles, 64 shuffles in all"). The body is unchanged from PR #4424 and the shuffle selectors that do carry meaning are already named (`EVEN_QUARTERS_0X88`, `ODD_QUARTERS_0XDD`).
