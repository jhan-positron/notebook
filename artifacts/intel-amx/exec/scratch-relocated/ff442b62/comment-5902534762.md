## Definitions that changed in the last 5 commits:

- Rows are per (forward class, pool worker, layer). `W` in `<class>_worker_<W>` is the pool worker index. The attention workers of a forward are the last `n_attn_workers` pool workers, so the first rows (the main helpers) read zero, and a row stays on one thread across forwards even when the plugin changes the split.
- T1 is taken by `forward_scope` inside `model::state::forward`: from after its perfetto slice to its return, logits and listener delivery inside.
- T3 and T4 are per (class, layer), observed by attention worker 0. The layer and totals leaves keep the `*_w0` keys. The worker leaves no longer carry them.
- T5 (`join_wait_cycles`) is measured in both join modes: the wait loop of the hardware join, and the stretches of failed polls on the peer workers' sections in the software-only join.
- `<class>_forwards` gains `listener_jobs` (token jobs with a listener: every generated token, and the last token of a prompt chunk), `kv_only_jobs` (token jobs that only stored their K and V), `n_attn_workers_min` and `n_attn_workers_max`. The three job counts of a forward are added together at its end.
- Under continuous batching a forward that carries any user's prompt chunk is `prompt_or_mixed`, so `decode_like` counts pure-decode forwards only.
- Every range guard of the hooks is a `TRON_ASSERT`. `note_token_path` is deleted.
- `t/t_attn_stats.cpp` has 19 cases. `full.hpp` is no longer changed by the PR.
