# Reader report: the PR-3 delphi-3bda chain scripts that PR-4 forks

## Short version

exec/pr3-20261012/chain2.sh runs ten "our half" steps (A, plugin, M1, M2, A2, A3, B, F, A0, head) that end in the all.done verdict, then five whole-machine steps (marker, serving down, D, E, C) that end in chain.done, with two mode switches that split the two halves into two runs. A PR-4 fork needs about 40 one-line edits (names, suffixes, the head arm's record, the case count, per-cell MUST_MATCH, a second campaign invocation for the FPGA cells, three new steps: deb, bench, the pre-check marker). Two facts outside the scripts matter most: the PR-3 branch gained a fourth commit dfafd13dce on 2026-10-12 (not yet on origin when read), and the fix1 check moved /var/tmp/jhan/tron-pr3 to that commit, so the head-arm reuse test of the fork fails for PR3_COMMIT=80bce9a08d and a rebuild of runtron.pr3 follows.

### 1.2 The modes

- START_NOT_BEFORE: a loop sleeps in slices of at most 1800 s until the time passes, printing "holding until ..." [131-135]. It holds the whole chain in both modes.
- END_BY: past_end [95] is tested before the whole-machine part [288], before every D cell [302], before E [311], before C [318], and in the WHOLE_MACHINE_ONLY wait loop [143]. A step that is past END_BY is skipped and its STEP line says so; the whole-machine part itself is not started past END_BY (chain.done=stopped-end-by) [288].
- DEADLINE_HHMM=0130: exported for D [300] and passed to E [312]. campaign.sh turns it into an epoch today, or tomorrow when that time has passed [campaign.sh:50-54], and stops a run or a wait at that epoch (return 2 -> finish deadline) [campaign.sh:82-91, 164-167, 241]. smoke.sh inherits the same functions [smoke.sh:17, 36-37]. Rule from PR3-handoff.md step 6: END_BY must never be later than the DEADLINE_HHMM time.
- STOP_AFTER_OUR_HALF=1: after the our-half verdict, when all.done = ok, writes chain.done = "our-half-done ok", STEP end, exit 0 [284].
- WHOLE_MACHINE_ONLY=1: skips the our-half steps [137]; loops until all.done reads "ok" (sleep 60 s) [140-145]; a non-empty other value aborts with chain.done="failed-our-half: <value>" [142]; past END_BY aborts with chain.done=stopped-end-by [143]; then the three binaries must be executable, else chain.done=failed-binary [146]; then STEP our-half "reused from the earlier run: ok; runtron.pr3 <sha256 16 chars> at <worktree HEAD>" [147].
