Clarify the packed V comments: index notation, parity, rank

Comment changes only, in v_vnni.hpp and kv_cache_fwd.hpp.

- "16 i" is written "16 * i" in the chunk() comment and in
  Note [Packed V layout].
- v_vnni_row::parity_ has its own comment: 0 for the even token of the
  pair, 1 for the odd token. It picks the element pair_base_[2 * dim +
  parity_] and, in chunk(), the low or the high 16 bits of every lane.
  pair_base_ keeps the first half of the old shared comment.
- The rank of an expression is defined at its first use (the number of
  indices: row[dim] takes one, view[token][dim] takes two).
- The plane shape is written [Rows tokens * Cols dimensions] in the
  v_vnni_view and the kv_cache_fwd.hpp comments.

only.
