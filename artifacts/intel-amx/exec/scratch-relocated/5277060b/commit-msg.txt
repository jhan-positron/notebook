VNNI K: one save_k for main and the main helpers

save_k takes the caller's worker index. Worker 0 is the main thread. It
opens the K store window, stores its share, joins the helpers and marks
the tokens. Workers 1 .. n_workers - 1 are the main helpers. Each waits
for its window, stores its share and leaves. save_k_helper is gone.

The generated run() calls save_k with worker index 0 and the generated
run_main_help calls the same save_k with the helper's worker_ix, at the
same statement position. The handwritten plugins keep their calls: the
defaults (worker 0 of 1) store every row on the calling thread.

The save_k test case of t_llama_unit also checks the K marks: every
job with KV work is marked exactly once and K alone does not complete
the token. A helper that ran main's mark loop would fail it.

Suggested by the PR 4424 review (bgamari, discussion r4065141577):
extend save_k with a worker_ix argument and call it consistently from
both sides instead of adding a second function.
