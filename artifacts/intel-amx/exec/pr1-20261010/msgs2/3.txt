t_llama_unit: keep main's split-vs-unsplit bounds for row-major K

The bounds of "apply and join page ranges / query splits match unsplit
page reductions" follow the K layout. Row-major K keeps the original
bounds (0.02 on an element of the weighted V sum, an absolute 2e-6 on
s_star at the first site). The VNNI layout takes 0.05 and a relative
1e-6: its scores differ in the last bits, so the bf16 conversion of the
PV weights rounds at a different point and one element reaches 0.022.
