k_vnni: the row operations check the token index

store_row and load_row assert token < Rows before computing the scatter
or gather address, as the packed-V operations do through
v_vnni_access::pair_base. copy_token inherits both checks. An index at
or above Rows would otherwise touch 64 dwords outside the 16 KiB plane.
The page accessors already check their index; the typed operations are
now self-checking as well.
