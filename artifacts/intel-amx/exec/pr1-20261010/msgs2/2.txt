t_phase1_integration: stage K rows through the scratch buffer

The populate case stores K rows with distinct per-(token, head,
element) patterns in one packed K plane per head. Its K callbacks gather
each head's row out of the plane into the scratch row that populate
supplies and return the scratch, as the scheduler's callback does for a
VNNI slot. After populate, every staged value is compared through the K
swizzle (position_ptr(t, h)[(e % nwords) * hbm_word_width + e / nwords])
for all tokens, heads and elements. populate collects every head's
pointer before set_position copies the rows, so a wrong per-head scratch
stride stages one head's data for every head and fails the comparison.
