tensor: the K VNNI layout, owner and views

h/tron/tensor/k_vnni.hpp defines typed access to a K plane stored in the
VNNI (Vector Neural Network Instructions) pair-interleaved layout: the
index map and its constants (namespace k_vnni), the layout gate
k_vnni::layout_on<head_size> (true for 128-dimension heads under
TRON_K_VNNI, false otherwise), the owner k_vnni_tensor, the view
k_vnni_view with a bounds-checked at(token, dim) and no element write,
the transfer-row aliases const_k_row_view / k_row_view with their
k_source_row / k_destination_row helpers, and the AVX-512 row operations
store_row (4 scatters), load_row (4 gathers) and copy_token.
detail::k_vnni_access is the one route from a view to the plane address.

h/tron/tensor/kv_cache_fwd.hpp forward-declares k_vnni_tensor and
k_vnni_view, so intrinsics-free headers can name them.
