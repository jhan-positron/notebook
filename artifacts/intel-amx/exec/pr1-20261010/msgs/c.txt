kernels: the AVX-512 reader of the VNNI K layout

h/tron/kernels/k_vnni.hpp adds k_vnni::qk_group, the AVX-512 score
reader of a VNNI K plane. One 64-byte masked load supplies one dimension
pair of 16 tokens. VDPBF16PS (bf16 query) or FMA on exactly widened
values (float query; an fp16 query is widened once) accumulates 16
scores at once. A group wider than 16 heads is scored in passes of 16.
A token whose bit is clear in the live mask is never read.

The software loop of self_attention.hpp records the relevant tokens of a
VNNI slot in a live mask and scores them after the loop with qk_group
through page::k_packed. A row-major slot keeps the per-token dotters.
The dense AMX page path is gated off for VNNI slots (temporary until the
VNNI AMX kernel lands), so every page of a VNNI slot takes the software
loop. The attn_stats comment and the amx_attn_iface diagram name the
reader.
