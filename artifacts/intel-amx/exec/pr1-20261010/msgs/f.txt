ci: build and test the CMake lane with TRON_K_VNNI=ON

The legacy CMake workflow (cmake-single-platform.yml) configures with
-DTRON_K_VNNI=ON next to -DTRON_AMX_DISPATCH=ON, so its test job runs
the whole suite on the VNNI layout. README.ci.md describes the option
and which builds set it.
