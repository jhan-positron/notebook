gof: the FPGA staging path reads K rows through the layout

page_info::k_head_fn takes a caller-owned scratch argument, as v_head_fn
does. The staging callback of full.hpp gathers a VNNI slot's token row
into that scratch with page::get_k_row, and returns the row-major
pointer otherwise. gof::populate passes a 64-byte-aligned K scratch per
head. The staging bytes and the HBM layout are unchanged. The three gof
tests take the new lambda arity.
