tests: t_k_vnni_layout and the t_llama_unit cases

t_k_vnni_layout checks the index map against hand-computed anchors, the
exactness and token independence of store_row / load_row / copy_token,
the reader against the row-major dotter for 4, 20 and 32 query heads
with bf16, float and fp16 queries over prefix, hole and single-token
masks, and the page accessors and page copies through set_k_row /
get_k_row and book::append.

t_llama_unit gains the packed-K typed-boundary probes, a save_k case
that stores 46 tokens over three pages in runs that start mid-block and
cross block and page boundaries and checks every row bit for bit and the
K marks, a compile check of software attention for 32 query heads on one
128-dimension K/V head, and the layout-agnostic accessors in every case
that wrote K through a row view. The split-vs-unsplit tolerance of
check_page_range_reductions widens to 5e-2 on v_stars and to a relative
1e-6 on s_stars, for the bf16 conversion of the PV weights at a
different running maximum.

t_amx_dispatch_dtype expects no dense kernel call for a VNNI slot.
t/CMakeLists.txt registers t_k_vnni_layout. config/test-benchmarks.json
carries its cost row and the refreshed rows of t_llama_unit and
t_amx_numerics.
