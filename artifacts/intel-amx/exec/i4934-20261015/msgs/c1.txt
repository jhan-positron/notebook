tensor: block_token_mask, the token bits of a page's VNNI blocks

k_vnni::block_token_bits(c) is the 16 page-offset bits of token block c.
k_vnni::block_token_mask(blocks) expands a page's block mask
(page::k_vnni_blocks, one bit per 16-token block) into the 64 token bits
of its VNNI blocks. page::k_block_bits delegates to the first one.

Why: the software scoring loop needs the token bits of the page's VNNI
blocks once per page visit, to split its live mask between the VNNI reader
and the row-major dotter after the loop instead of testing the layout of
every token inside it.
