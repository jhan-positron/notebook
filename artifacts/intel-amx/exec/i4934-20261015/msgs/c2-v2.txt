attention: score the row-major rows after the live-mask loop

On a VNNI slot the software scoring loop of apply_page_tok now does one
thing per relevant token: it sets the token's bit in the live mask. After
the loop the page's block mask splits the live mask in two parts. The
tokens of VNNI blocks go to k_vnni::qk_group in one call. The tokens of
row-major blocks go to the row-major dotters, one row at a time, and the
dotters are built only when such a row exists. The dotter arithmetic for
a row-major row is unchanged.

Why: the loop built four dotters on every page visit (sixteen 512-bit
loads of the query group) and tested the layout of every relevant token,
whether or not the page had a row-major row. With the AMX kill switch
every page visit runs this loop. A probe that only deferred the dotters
measured about two points of decode throughput at tp2 with a prompt of
1024 tokens; the per-token tests are the one cost that grows with the
loop iterations of a prefill (issue #4934).

The kill-switch contract clause of Note [AMX attention dispatch] names
the AVX-512 readers the switch runs, and item 3 of Note [K VNNI storage]
names where the dotter loop runs.
