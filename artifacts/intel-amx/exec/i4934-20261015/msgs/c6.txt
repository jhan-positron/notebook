tests: the software loop scores a mixed page as its two readers do

A new case of t_amx_dispatch_dtype drives apply_page_range with the AMX
path off over a page whose rows 0..31 are recorded (two VNNI blocks under
TRON_K_VNNI, two row-major blocks) and whose K rows carry values: one
token's row equals the query, every other row is the query divided by 8
with its own signs. The softmax state's m_star must equal, bit for bit,
the largest score of the page computed through the reader of each
token's block (k_vnni::qk_group or the row-major dotter) times the
scale. Six sections: the dominant row in a row-major block and in a VNNI
block, for the host_bf16, host and host_fp16 executors.

Why: no test scored a page with VNNI blocks and row-major blocks through
the production loop with a bf16 query, and no test could tell the two
readers apart (their fp32 add orders differ). This one fails when a
row-major row is scored through the reader or a VNNI block through the
dotter.

exercise_page takes its shape as one struct (page_shape) instead of
five arguments.
