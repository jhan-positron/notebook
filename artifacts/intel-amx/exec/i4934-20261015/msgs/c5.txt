model: convert the completed K blocks on the app pool at the forward end

convert_pending_k_blocks converts a queue of k_convert_parallel_min or
more blocks on the app-pool workers (thread_pool::parallel_for, one
strided share per worker) and joins them before it returns; a shorter
queue stays on the main thread. The call keeps its place at the forward
end, after the staging drained and before the logit delivery reuses the
pool: the listener thunks of that delivery take the tree lock on pool
workers and may move or free books. The queue holds every block once,
which the parallel branch needs.

Why: the conversion of a user's completed block is one in-place
transpose per layer and KV head, about 300 of them per completion for
a 36-layer model with 8 KV heads. They ran serially on the main thread
while every pool worker was idle, on the critical path of the step.

A new section of the save_k case completes every block of three pages
in one pass, so the queue takes the parallel branch, and checks every
row, every block bit and the empty queue.
