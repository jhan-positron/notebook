CMakePresets: the deb preset compiles the VNNI K layout

Words used here. CMake is the build configuration tool. A preset is a
named CMake build configuration. The deb preset of CMakePresets.json
builds the released Debian package. AMX (Advanced Matrix Extensions) is
the tile instruction set. TRON_AMX_DISPATCH is the switch that compiles
the AMX attention kernels. VNNI (Vector Neural Network Instructions) is
the pair-interleaved layout of Note [K VNNI storage] in
h/tron/models/kv_cache.hpp. TRON_K_VNNI is the switch that stores the K
cache (the attention keys) of 128-dimension heads in that layout.
AVX-512 is the 512-bit vector instruction set.

The deb preset sets TRON_K_VNNI ON next to TRON_AMX_DISPATCH. The
released package then stores the K cache of 128-dimension heads in the
VNNI layout on every host. The layout does not depend on AMX. Only the
reader does: the AMX kernel on hosts with AMX, the AVX-512 reader
elsewhere. Every other preset leaves the option at its default OFF.
README.ci.md says so in its TRON_K_VNNI paragraph.
