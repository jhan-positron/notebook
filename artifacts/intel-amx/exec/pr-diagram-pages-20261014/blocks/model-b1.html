MODEL-B1. Data model: the minibatch, the K store window, the pending list, and the K buffer

A batch holds the items of one minibatch (token jobs), each at one page offset. The PR adds batch::k_store_window_t (in common.hpp: the claim counter, the open/finished counters, the unit table and the helpers' window counters) and state::pending_k_blocks_ (the blocks to convert at the forward end). Every arrow says how many. The worked example is a 40-item window with 3 workers (main and 2 main helpers).
