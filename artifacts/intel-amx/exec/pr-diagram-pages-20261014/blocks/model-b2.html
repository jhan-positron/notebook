MODEL-B2. Function model: save_k, its helpers, and what they call in the page

save_k (two overloads) calls save_k_impl. save_k_impl decides the sharing (k_store_shared), cuts the units (k_store_cut_units), stores the runs (store_k_block) and records the rows (note_k_row_saved). convert_pending_k_blocks runs at the forward end. The generated plugins call save_k from run() (worker 0) and run_main_help (the helpers). Every cell says in one line what the function does.
