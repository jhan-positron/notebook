K_VNNI-B2. Type model: owner, views, the one access route, and the transfer rows

k_vnni_tensor owns the bytes. k_vnni_view is the only way to name a plane, and detail::k_vnni_access::plane the only way to get its address. The transfer rows const_k_row_view / k_row_view carry one token's 128 values in dimension order. The diagram shows what each type can and cannot do and who is a friend of whom.
