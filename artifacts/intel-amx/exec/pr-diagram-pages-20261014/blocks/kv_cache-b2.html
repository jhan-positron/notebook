KV_CACHE-B2. One 16-token block, two layouts in the same 4 KiB

The K plane of one (slot, KV head, page) is 16 panels of 1 KiB. A block owns four of them, 4 KiB apart. The PR lets those four panels hold either 16 row-major token rows or the VNNI form, converts between the two in place (k_vnni.hpp), and records which form a block holds in the page's block bit.
