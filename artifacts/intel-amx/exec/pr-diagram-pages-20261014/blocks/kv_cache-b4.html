KV_CACHE-B4. Sequencing model: the life of one 16-token block

A block of one storage slot moves through four states, plus a fifth state (records no row bits: stays row-major) under hardware attention. The upper path is the decode case (one row per step). The arc over the top is the prefill case (a whole block at once). The lower path is the hardware-attention policy, under which a block completed by short runs never converts. Clears and copies move a block back.
