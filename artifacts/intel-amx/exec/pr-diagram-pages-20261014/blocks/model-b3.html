MODEL-B3. Sequencing model: one shared save_k window, step by step

The ordered steps of one window, each citing the line that does it. Main cuts the units, resets the counters and opens the window (release on opened). Each helper waits for its window number (acquire on opened). Every thread claims units with fetch_add on next_unit and stores them. Each helper increments finished (release). Main joins (acquire on finished) under a watchdog. Main records the rows and queues the completed blocks. Main marks the tokens and credits the GOFs.
