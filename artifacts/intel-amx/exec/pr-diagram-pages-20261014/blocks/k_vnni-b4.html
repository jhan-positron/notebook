K_VNNI-B4. Sequencing model: the bytes of one block through the operations

One 16-token block of the worked example (block 2 of the plane of slot 5, KV head 0, page 7) passes through store_row_major, block_to_vnni, store_row and load_row, and back through block_to_rows. The diagram shows which operation touches which lines of the block's 4 KiB and in which form. A solid vermillion box is the conversion of a complete block (block_to_vnni here, convert_pending_k_blocks in B5, k_block_to_vnni_at in K9); the dashed vermillion chip of the legend is the complete block that waits for it.
