== k_vnni-b1: K_VNNI-B1. Data model: one K plane, its panels, pair rows and the index map
INTRO: A plane is K for one KV head of one page: 64 tokens x 128 dimensions of bf16 = 16 KiB, the same bytes as the row-major plane. It is 16 panels of 1 KiB, indexed by dimension step s and token block c. A panel is 16 pair rows of 64 bytes. A pair row holds one dimension pair of 16 tokens. Every arrow says how many. A kv_block holds one K plane (k) and one V plane (v). A plane is K (the key matrix) for one KV head (key/value head) of one page (64 tokens of KV storage): 64 tokens x 128 dimensions of bf16 (16-bit floats) = 16 KiB, owned by k_vnni_tensor.
