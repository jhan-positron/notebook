Short version. The codex comment is right in substance: PR 0's third commit puts -DTRON_PAGE_SHARE_COUNTERS=ON on the CI build job's configure line, that job bundles its runtron as the benchmark artifact, and the benchmark job runs that bundled binary, so after merge every CI benchmark would measure an instrumented program. Two corrections: the clean-build fallback codex mentions is a rare corner case, not a coin flip, and the stronger reason to act is one codex did not name: the nightly golden baseline is a clean build, so every report's golden section would compare instrumented against clean, permanently. Recommended fix: take the flag out of the build job and switch it on in the weekly coverage preset instead; the hooks still compile in CI, and the benchmark, fallback and golden binaries agree again.

Words used here

  • PR 0: pull request #4267 "page_share_counters" (branch jhan-page-share-counters): instrumentation that counts how many query tokens visit each KV page (a 64-token block of attention keys and values) in the software attention loop; compiled in only by the CMake option TRON_PAGE_SHARE_COUNTERS, default OFF. Its third commit (8a867e03a3) added the option to the CI build job so that the hooks are compiled somewhere in CI (jhan's decision 2 of 2026-09-07, "option B").
  • runtron: tron's command-line tool, the program the CI benchmarks run. rinzler: the production server.
  • benchmark artifact: gen/runtron plus its build-tree shared libraries, bundled by bin/ci/ci-bundle-artifact.sh into /scratch/tron-build/<tron_sha7>/artifacts.tar.zst, where tron_sha7 is a hash of every git-tracked file's content (the "source hash").
  • --prebuilt / --paranoid: two modes of bin/ci/benchmark_save.py: use the extracted artifact, or make clean and rebuild runtron from source.
  • golden baseline: the nightly multi-repeat benchmark (benchmark-golden.yml, 08:00 UTC) used as the stable comparison in every benchmark report.
  • hooks: the five #ifdef TRON_PAGE_SHARE_COUNTERS blocks in h/tron/models/self_attention.hpp (lines 1290-1358) inside apply_page_range, the per-page software attention loop.
