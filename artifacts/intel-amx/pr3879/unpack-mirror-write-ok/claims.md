# Second snippet: the "allocated at construction" bullet of Note [K mirror parallel arena], kv_cache.hpp ~581-591
N1. The bullet reads: "allocated at construction only when the model is AMX-eligible - bf16 activations and an attention kernel of the AMX shape (amx_mirror_eligible over the plugin's attention kernels, i.e. amx_attn::eligible - the same gate as the dispatch; the save_k write-through fills every bf16 head-128 plane the arena holds, amx_attn::mirror_write_ok, because a read-only consumer of another query ratio may share the writer's slot) AND amx_attn::available(); when absent (ineligible model, non-AMX host, kill switch, or allocation failure) it is null and every consumer null-checks and falls back (fail-soft, never fatal)." N2.
