2. tron-code-review

Coverage: protect the production conversion call

Location: h/tron/models/model.hpp:1847.

The new save tests invoke state.convert_pending_k_blocks() themselves. Their assertions verify the helper but do not protect its call from the real forward path. [t/t_llama_unit.cpp:4066]

state.convert_pending_k_blocks();
check_blocks(/*converted=*/true);

The existing heterogeneous forward test creates four live tokens. It checks forward statistics and does not complete a 16-token block or assert conversion. [t/heterogeneous_scheduler_compile.cpp:914]

Consequence: deleting the production call, or moving it before the staging barrier, is not distinguished by the new conversion assertions. This is a coverage finding, not an observed production failure.

Smallest remedy: extend an existing forward test to complete a previously partial block. After return, check the packed-state bit and retained K values. Use the production entry point throughout. For the ordering contract, hold a staging reader until the barrier and verify its row-major reads before release.

Discriminating check: removing the production conversion call must fail the new forward test. This mutation was proposed, not run.

Inspected ordering

Workers join→Staging drains→Queued blocks convert→Logits delivered

Control-flow order only. No timing or duration is implied. [h/tron/models/model.hpp:1836]

Pending entries retain the physical storage offset and its layout-state index. Scheduler-owned books keep pages alive through the forward pass. EAGLE resets its view after that pass. Staging copies source rows into owned transfer buffers before submitting the transfer. The later transfer reads those buffers, not the K plane being converted.

Conditions inspected: layout on and off, AMX enabled and disabled, hardware and software operation policies, empty and partial runs, mixed layouts, append and reuse, and restored storage. This was source inspection. No new hardware workload, EAGLE model execution or sanitizer run was performed.
