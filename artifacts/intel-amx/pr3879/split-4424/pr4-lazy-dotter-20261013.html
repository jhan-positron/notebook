Deferred-dotter probe with AMX disabled, 2026-10-13

Short version. With AMX disabled (TRON_AMX_DISABLE=1), PR-4's binary is 3.7 %, 3.1 % and 1.2 % slower than its parent PR-3 in the three CPU cells. A probe build that constructs the dotters of a page only at its first row-major row recovers 2.3, 1.6 and 0.2 percentage points of that. A gap of 1.5 %, 1.5 % and 1.0 % to the parent remains, with no measured cause. With AMX enabled the probe build costs nothing measurable.

headoff (parent PR-3)newoff (PR-4)lazyoff (probe build)

Each panel has its own axis, which does not start at zero. TPS = decode tokens per second per user, mean of 3 repetitions (qwen3-4b, 8 users, 256 generated tokens, software attention USE_HW_ATTN=0, on the whole Granite Rapids test machine with serving down). "beyond" and "inside" refer to the band: twice the larger sample standard deviation of the pair, with a floor of 0.40 TPS. In the 8,192-token cell every band is the floor, which is 2.6 % of that cell's 15 TPS.

Words used here

dotter
The software routine that scores one row-major K row against a query row. The PR-4 software loop built the dotters of every page before knowing whether any row needed them. The probe build (commit ca6456b7dc on ea94e95a4e, not in the PR) builds them at the first row-major row of a page.
AMX, TRON_AMX_DISABLE
AMX is the Intel tile instruction set. TRON_AMX_DISABLE=1 is the kill switch that turns the AMX kernels off at run time. The "off" arms use it.
head, new, lazy
head = PR-3 (dfafd13dce), new = PR-4 (ea94e95a4e), lazy = the probe build. headoff, newoff, lazyoff = the same binaries with the kill switch.
paired t, band
The paired t over the 3 repetition pairs. The band is the campaign's threshold for a change: twice the larger sample standard deviation of the pair, at least 0.40 TPS.
