## Words used here

- PR: pull request. PR-1: PR #4868, the layout PR, open at 82d05e94be. PR-2: the AMX kernel PR this handoff builds. AMX: Intel Advanced Matrix Extensions, the tile instruction set.
- Arm: one binary with one environment setting in a smoke (a token-identity run) or a cell (a performance run). The arms of the PR-2 campaign: base (main at 72308ec90c), head (PR-1's binary), headoff (head with the kill switch TRON_AMX_DISABLE=1), new (PR-2's binary), newoff (new with the kill switch), new2 (a repeat of new).
- The reader: PR-1's AVX-512 loop that scores K stored in the VNNI layout. The temporary gate: PR-1's clause that sends every page of a VNNI slot to the reader; PR-2 removes it. The query pack: the step that copies the query rows into the AMX operand buffer.
- The band: twice the larger standard deviation of two arms, never below 0.4 TPS (decode tokens per second per user) or 0.15 s of TTFT (time to first token).
- The chain: the delphi-3bda build-and-test script the executor forks from exec/pr1-20261010/chain2.sh and chain3.sh. The summarizer: exec/sync4424-20261002/summarize.py, which turns a campaign's results into summary.md.

## 2. Changes applied to PR2-handoff.md

### 2.1 Step 6 E (point 1), with one addition

The E row now reads: ARMS="base new newoff head headoff", WEDPERF_ARMS="base new newoff head headoff", WEDPERF_PAIRS="new:base new:head newoff:new newoff:headoff head:headoff". The addition: headoff (PR-1's binary with the kill switch) is a fifth arm from the start, not a diagnostic to add if a discrepancy occurs. Reason: a later diagnostic run needs a second whole-machine window (serving down, Bill's marker, the lease), and the fifth arm of E together with the fifth arm of D costs about 15 min (est.) inside the first window. The two pairs it gives, newoff:headoff (both binaries with AMX off) and head:headoff (the kill switch inside PR-1's binary), separate the two effects codex names.

### 2.2 Step 6 D and section 5.4 (point 2)

The D row now says what the script's verdict does and does not check, and requires a validator in the chain fork: after each smoke, every arm has a "smoke <arm> rc=0" line in smoke.txt and every arm's <arm>.tokens file holds exactly 256 tokens. A cell passes only when the validator and smoke.done both say ok. The validator is tested once before the run with two synthetic fixtures that must fail (a 1-token file; one arm with 255 tokens) and one complete fixture that must pass. Section 5.4 gains the matching first bullet (rc 0 and 256 tokens per arm, whatever the verdict says). Section 8 (traps) names both script facts.

### 2.3 Section 5.5 (point 3)

The sentence "newoff vs head: expected inside the band (both arms run the reader for every page); a difference beyond the band is a finding about the measurement, not about the code" is replaced by three bullets: newoff vs new (unchanged); newoff vs headoff (both binaries with AMX off, both run the reader and pack no query, expected inside the band, a difference beyond the band is investigated before it is attributed to variation); head vs headoff (the kill switch inside PR-1's binary: head also allocates the query-pack slots, enters the tile region and packs queries for the path its gate never takes; PR-1's measured numbers at 82d05e94be are the expectation).
