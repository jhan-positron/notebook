Words used here

tron, deb, the nightly
tron is the inference program. A deb is its Debian package. The nightly is the nightly test job that installs the newest deb on the Granite Rapids test machine and runs its performance harness.
PR 4424, PR-4 (PR #4901)
PR 4424 is the original VNNI K pull request. PR-4 is the last of the four pull requests that replace it.
TPS, TTFT
TPS: generated tokens per second as the harness reports them. TTFT: time to first token in milliseconds.
paired t, resolved
The paired t statistic over the 20 round pairs (target minus base, round means). A result is resolved when the absolute t is at least 2.26 and the absolute difference is at least 1 % (TTFT also at least 1 ms).
tp2, tp4, cell
Tensor parallelism over 2 or 4 engines. A cell is one model, one user count and one prompt length.
VmHWM, VmRSS
Peak and current resident memory of the serving processes, summed over engines, in MiB.
