## Status after the four stack PRs (2026-10-14)

**Short version.** To-do item 1 is done: two forced runs confirmed the near-tie explanation, and nothing larger than the add-order envelope was found. Item 2, the kill-switch contract, is still undecided. Item 3, a model-level bound, has not been started. The stack PRs now point here for this topic instead of restating it.

**Words used here.** The stack is PR #4956 (VNNI-packed K layout and the VNNI reader), PR #4957 (the AMX kernel for that layout), PR #4958 (shared 16-token block saves) and PR #4959 (row-major tail block and the `deb` preset), each based on the preceding one, replacing PR #4424. `base` is runtron built from `main` at `72308ec90c` with row-major K. `new` is the PR's head with `TRON_K_VNNI` and `TRON_AMX_DISPATCH` on. `newoff` runs `new` with the kill switch. A ulp here is one bf16 step at the size of the score.

### Item 1: the measurement, done twice

- **PR #4957, head `0ee8671d35`, 2026-10-11**, forced run on `new`'s 256-token sequence of the CPU prompt-1,024 cell. At the step that predicts generated token 247, `new` scores token ids 13 and 624 equal (26.625 each in bf16) and picks 13. `newoff` scores them one bf16 step (0.125) apart in favour of 624. The two paths differ in their scores at every step, but only that step flips the top token. At the other 255 steps the margin between the two best tokens is 3.9 at the median (39 bf16 steps). Near ties are common: 12 of the 256 steps have a margin of at most 2 bf16 steps, 2 of them exact ties, and only this one flipped. `newoff` and the PR #4956 binary produce identical scores at all 256 steps.
- **PR #4959, implementation `ea94e95a4e`, probe of 2026-10-13 (record `p83-20261013`)**, every other arm fed `new`'s tokens. At generated token 83, `base` under CPU attention and the PR #4958 binary under FPGA attention sit on an exact tie between vocabulary tokens 258 and 1499, which their tie-break resolves to 258. `new` scores 1499 ahead by 1 to 2 ulps. At generated token 247 the kill-switch arm reverses a 1-ulp margin. Every other step agrees on the top token, with scores within ulp-level rounding.

So each observed divergence is an exact tie or a 1-to-2-ulp margin at one step, as Note [AMX attention dispatch] predicts. The September observations above (PR #4424, steps 46, 5 and 52) were made without score records and are superseded by these two runs.

### Item 2: the kill-switch contract, still open

With `TRON_K_VNNI` on, the kill switch gives an AMX-off run of the same binary in all four PRs, not the tokens of a clean binary. The two options stand as written above: (a) add "with `TRON_K_VNNI` off" to item 4 of Note [AMX attention dispatch], or (b) keep a row-major build as the deployment rollback artifact and document it. Which arm's rounding is preferable at the near ties is also undecided. Attributing a 1-to-2-ulp difference to one operation would need a per-layer comparison of the intermediates, which has not been run.

### Item 3: a model-level bound

Not started. The PR #4957 forced run is one data point for it: the per-step score differences between the AMX path and the reader path stay at ulp level, and the only top-token change is the exact tie at token 247.
