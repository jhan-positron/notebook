PR 4868 description: what each section does, and three ways to halve it

Measured on the live body of 2026-10-13 (after the "VNNI-packed" wording edit). Word counts are wc -w counts. "est." marks a planned size, not a measurement.

Short version. The body of PR 4868 has 6,191 words in 20 sections, about 21 times the median body of the last 60 merged tron PRs (300 words). Two sections (The changes and Verification) hold 45 % of the section words, and the same facts are stated in up to four places. Of the three designs below (about 2,800 to 3,200 words est.), I recommend Design A, which merges the duplicates and keeps every fact once.

3. Three cutting designs

Each design states its principle, its per-group targets (est.), which handoff rule it changes, and what a reviewer loses. The dumbbells show the current size (gray) and the target (colored) per group. The number above each line is the cut.

Only the Short version keeps its size in every design. The changes and Verification give up the most words in all three.

Design A: merge the duplicates, keep every fact once (recommended)

6,191 → about 2,920 words est. (-53 %). Reading time 26 → 12 min est.

Principle. The skeleton shrinks from 20 sections to 11. Each fact is stated in one place. Tables stay. The prose around a table says only what the table does not. Measurements and Verification become one section, because both are records at one head and the machine, the date, the two commit ids and the model are stated twice today.

Outcome

Design A was executed on 2026-10-13. The live body went from 6,202 words to 4,096 words (−34 %), in 7 top-level sections. Two things differ from the plan above. Section 4 is "How to review this PR" (the Q-and-A section of the PR-description skill) in place of a tightened "The changes". And the merged Verification section measures 1,632 words, not the 800 words est. of the plan, because its five tables alone hold 614 words and the plan's estimate did not count them. Open items kept every number (838 words against 470 est.), and the glossary gained rows for kv_mul, the rollback contract and the four binaries (514 words against 300 est.). A further cut to about 3,100 words would need one of: the earlier PR 4424 numbers out of Open items (about 90 words), the builds table folded into one sentence (about 60), or the "what the tables say" bullets halved (about 150), with the rest from the Words table.
