5. Method

  1. Corpus: union of gh search prs --reviewed-by and --commenter for Wado-posi across positron-ai, minus his own PRs: 115 PRs, 334 reviews, 243 inline and 11 conversation comments, every endpoint paginated. One digest per PR with hunks and replies.
  2. 30 classifier agents labelled all 273 texts (concern, fit to the 13 old checks, detection sketch, evidence read, form, severity, author outcome). 202 texts fit none of the old checks.
  3. 3 taxonomists (by mechanism, by evidence, by delta to the skill) and 1 judge merged them into 35 clusters.
  4. Per cluster: 1 drafter, 3 refuters (precision on 15 silently approved PR diffs, verbatim facts at the cited commit, executability on an originating PR), 1 tightener. All 32 drafted checks survived. 6 quotes that were author replies were removed.
  5. 1 critic (overlaps, codex coverage, contradictions), 32 compressors, 1 style writer, 3 plain-English checkers, 1 consistency reviewer. 235 agents in total (34 + 162 + 33 in three workflows, plus 6 standalone).
