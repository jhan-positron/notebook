  • Instance placement. The plan says runtron with --instance 1,2 (four cards) for the tp2 model; the proven tp2 placement of the earlier campaigns was used instead: --instance 2,4 with cards 90:00.0 and 93:00.0 and worker cores of socket 1 only (our half). The idle bursts of an engine that has been idle for hours are far apart (est. about one hour, from the growing interval that lib-guard documents: 300, 450, 675, 1013 s and so on).
