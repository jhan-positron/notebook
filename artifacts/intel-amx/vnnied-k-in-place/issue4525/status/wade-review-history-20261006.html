Runtime and performance

  • Trace physical reads, buffer lifetime, and the exact completion object.
  • Check hardware bounds, padding, and alignment at the real consumer.
  • Count repeated work; require measurements before claiming a time cost.

Batch work review · Lock ownership review

Tests and instrumentation

  • Verify that fakes observe transfer arguments and tests use the changed production route.
  • Keep independent expected results and distinct failure observations.
  • Check every promised metric path and stable worker identity.

Test boundary review · Missing timer path

Corrections and severity

  • Read later replies before treating a suggestion as precedent.
  • Keep nits and approved follow-ups separate from defects.
  • Replace forced consolidation and inferred approval gates with evidence-based questions.
