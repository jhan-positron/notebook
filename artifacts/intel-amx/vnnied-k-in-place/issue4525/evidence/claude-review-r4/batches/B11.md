### G19 [round-3 NEW finding, severity MINOR] for_each_active_slot visits logical slots, not physical; 'logical alias' is undefined and cannot occur in a fixed layout
- Where in the round-3 design: 6. Arena construction without clearing, heterogeneous bullet (design-r3.txt:350)
- Relates to earlier id(s): M04 N25
- Kind: ambiguity | found by lenses: behavior-arena, disposition-checker
- Claim: The heterogeneous bullet says for_each_active_slot 'dispatches the physical slot to its compile-time geometry' and ends with a caution about a 'logical alias' that the design never defines. In the code, for_each_active_slot iterates slot indices into kv_slot_specs and keeps only slot < logical_slot_count(); its comment calls these logical slots; callers form the physical offset with kv_storage_offset(slot), as fill_random does. A fixed (heterogeneous) layout maps each logical slot to exactly one physical offset: make_kv_slot_offsets computes one prefix per slot and storage_location reads offsets.values[offset.i]. The alias sentence is carried over from round 2 and no earlier round raised it.
