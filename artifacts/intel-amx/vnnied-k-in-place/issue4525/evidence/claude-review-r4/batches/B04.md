- Relates to earlier id(s): N14 N03

- Claim: The design states type constraints for the new owners and views (even rows, columns divisible by chunk_size, T is bf16 or const bf16, a 16,384-byte packed-K owner) but never says how they are expressed. The natural C++20 form, a requires-clause on the class template, breaks two other rules of the same design.
