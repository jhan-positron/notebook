> [!NOTE]
> **Medium Risk**
> Touches the HW attention GOF backfill path and changes when data is DMA’d, which can impact correctness and HW/SW boundary behavior despite being a small diff.
> 
> **Overview**
> Tightens `backfill_shard` to **only DMA/backfill GOFs that are fully saved**, by gating on `g->save_count >= 2 * gof_tokens` before populating and transferring.
> 
> This prevents partially-saved GOFs from being backfilled with stale page data and from incorrectly increasing the “complete GOF” count, improving correctness of the HW attention vs SW fallback split.
