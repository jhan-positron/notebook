> [!NOTE]
> **Medium Risk**
> Touches low-level DMA queue state tracking and introduces new KV-cache transfer paths; bugs here can cause incorrect completion detection or HBM writes, though changes are localized and covered by new tests.
> 
> **Overview**
> Adds a new *cacheblock-centric* hardware-attention KV transfer API: `hwcacheblock` becomes polymorphic with `k_hwcacheblock`/`v_hwcacheblock` `xfer()` + `xfer_blocking()` methods, plus `hwcacheblock::isdone()`/`wait()` helpers, and documents this as the preferred replacement for legacy `save_*_to_hardware` flows.
> 
> Hardens DMA completion accounting by making `DmaQueue` wrap-count tracking atomic (with CAS-based wrap detection), reserving queue storage to avoid vector moves, and adding `dma_xfer_state::operator<` to enable ordering/sorting of transfer states. Updates and expands `t_hwattention` to validate new constructors, whole-cacheblock transfers (including polymorphic usage), and fixes several test deallocation sizes.
