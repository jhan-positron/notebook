> [!NOTE]
> **High Risk**
> Touches core KV-cache save path, scheduler traversal, and HW-attention planning/execution; regressions could break correctness or performance and add subtle concurrency issues (atomic completion tracking, async DMA coordination). Also changes device job interfaces (`fill_attn_job_template`) and join logic across SW/HW paths.
> 
> **Overview**
> Implements a **Phase 1/2 HW-attention refactor**: GOFs are consolidated into a new `hardware::gof` with captured `page_info` accessors, and per-query `gof_job` entries are produced by the scheduler traversal and passed to the model as spans for *tree-unaware* `gof_rodeo` DMA dispatch.
> 
> Reworks traversal and edge state to support shard/ranged-mask integration: edges gain a `dirty` flag, real `edge_shards()` caching, shard creation/backfill during traversal, and the scheduler now emits `hw_plan_entry` spans consumed by `construct_hw_plan` to build a new `hw_workload` (passes grouped by device and split into ≤4 shards/pass).
> 
> Updates the forward/attention pipeline to use the plan-based HW path: `save_k/save_v` now track per-token KV completion via atomics on pages and trigger `gof_rodeo`; `self_attention` prepares/launches multiple HW passes (multi-Q `fill_attn_job_template` API), merges HW pass scratchpads during joins, and removes the previous HW validation/observer plumbing and `hw_kv_section`-based planning.
