> [!NOTE]
> **Medium Risk**
> Touches core scheduler shard propagation and HW-attention eligibility logic, which can impact correctness/performance of KV offload across long prompts. Changes are localized but sensitive to boundary conditions (split points, DMA progress, first-shard handling).
> 
> **Overview**
> Fixes a repeated-iteration assertion caused by inconsistent shard ordering/positioning when prompts are ^@31024 tokens and edges are split.
> 
> When splitting an edge, shards are now partitioned between the new intermediate edge and the child edge based on whether their covered range ends before the split point (instead of always keeping shards on the child). During traversal, shard push entries now use *edge-relative* positions and skip pushing when a shard^Es DMA progress has not yet reached the current edge^Es base, preventing invalid `clamped >= edge_base` assumptions.
