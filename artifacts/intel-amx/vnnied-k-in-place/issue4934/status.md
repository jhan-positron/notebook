## Words used here

- kill switch: `TRON_AMX_DISABLE=1`, the AMX kernels off at run time inside the same binary.
- the loop: the software scoring loop of `apply_page_tok` in `h/tron/models/self_attention.hpp`.
- the reader: `k_vnni::qk_group`, the AVX-512 routine that scores a query group against the VNNI blocks of a page.
- the dotter: `dotter<bf16, 128>` (`h/tron/kernels/dotter.hpp`), the per-token dot product over one row-major K row.
- tail block: an incomplete 16-token K block kept row-major inside its own bytes (PR #4901), converted in place at the forward end (`convert_pending_k_blocks`, `h/tron/models/model.hpp`).
- H1..H4: the leads (H1 per-token layout tests in the loop, H2 dotters built per page visit, H3 the serial forward-end conversion, H4 the per-token row-bit loop in `save_k`).
