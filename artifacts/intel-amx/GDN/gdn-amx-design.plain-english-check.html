Plain-English check of the GDN and AMX design page

2026-11-21. Rule set: plain-english-experiment. Checked text: the page that the reviewer accepted in round 2.

Short version

  • The reviewed page had 354 violations of the experimental plain-English rules.
  • Most were labels at the start of bullets, and bullets with more than 3 sentences.
  • The rewritten page has none left, and no measured or predicted number changed.
Words used here
  • violation: one place where the text breaks one rule of the rule set.
  • label: a word or short phrase at the start of a bullet. It names the topic, for example "Clock." in bold.
  • counting script: check_lengths.py of the rule set. It counts words per sentence and sentences per bullet.
  • writer, reader: an agent that rewrote one part of the page, and an agent that compared old and new text.
  • generator: the python scripts that produce the page from the measured data.
  • est.: an estimate.

1. Result by rule

  • Rules 2, 5 and 7 had no violation of their own. A sentence that joined two claims was also too long, so it counts under rule 3.
  • The counts come from the 8 writers. The counting script alone found 206 of them, because it cannot see labels, idioms or terms.

3. How the rewrite was checked

  1. 8 writers rewrote one part of the page each, on a private copy of the generator.
  2. The counting script ran on the merged page, with table cells and captions included. It printed nothing.
  3. 4 readers compared old and new text sentence by sentence, and 1 reader checked the layout in a browser.
  4. The readers reported 20 problems. 19 are corrected, and 1 is kept on purpose (see below).
  5. The page regenerates byte for byte, and the prediction data file is unchanged.
  • The readers found 3 changes of meaning. All 3 came from a split that separated a condition from its claim. They are corrected.
  • Kept on purpose: one label of figure 13 lost the word "the". Its parenthesis had 9 words, and the limit is 8.
  • Five names occur once more or once less, for example "Part 0" and "AVX-512". No measured or predicted number changed.

4. Text that the rewrite added

  • New entries in "Words used here": increment, and chain path.
  • New inline definitions: FPGA attention, pass wall, step wall, and PyTorch.
  • New definitions in the sources: checkpoint, replay, HW-attention, ready and pending pages, and CUDA.
  • Three "(est.)" marks. A split sentence needs the mark on both halves.
