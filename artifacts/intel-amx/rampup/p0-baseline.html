Each attention worker is ≈67–68% busy, and its busy time is almost entirely attention compute; the remaining ≈32% is uninstrumented spin-waiting (Q-channel and K/V waits have no spans in stock tron — the PoC's attn_profile exists precisely to split those). Conclusion: the estimate is confirmed and sharpened — at 8 users / ctx 2048, attention compute is the dominant use of the attention workers (13 of ≈19 worker-cores), so the whole-path AMX kernel attacks the right cost.
