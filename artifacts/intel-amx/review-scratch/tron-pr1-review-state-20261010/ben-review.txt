Finding B1 — Reuse the K transfer-row helper for the conversion buffer (P3, optional readability)
Location: h/tron/models/model.hpp:3117-3119.

Proposed inline comment:
Could this use k_destination_row<geometry.kv.head_size>(row).set(kout)? The new helper already names the contiguous bf16 K transfer row. This spelling makes the reader decode its element type, alignment, DMA flag, and shape again before recognizing the same row passed to set_k_row below. The helper's defaults preserve this stack buffer's current flags.
