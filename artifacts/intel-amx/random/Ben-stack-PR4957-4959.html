1. The five lessons of PR 5047

PR 5047 is Ben's rewrite of PR 4956 (the VNNI K layout and its readers). Its two commits show what he wants from the rest of the stack.

P1
One owner of the K layout: the cache. His second commit moved the scoring (page::score_k), the AMX attempt (page::try_score_k_amx) and the activation-row conversion (set_k_row with an expression) into the page. The count of the layout flag k_vnni::layout_on went from 2/1/1 to 0/0/0 in self_attention.hpp, model.hpp and full.hpp.
P2
No public raw K views: the eight page::k() overloads and page::k_packed are gone, page::kv_block is private, and tests go through t/kv_cache_helpers.hpp.
P3
One control flow per function: his first commit made the token-selection loop only select tokens into a 64-bit mask. Scoring happens once after the loop. The placeholder lambdas for the other layout are gone.
P4
A call site asks the cache and takes the answer: apply_dense_amx_page returns false when the cache declines at run time, instead of a compile-time layout clause at the call site.
P5
His own words in the PR body: "The previously-proposed implementation was a bit spaghetti for my liking."
