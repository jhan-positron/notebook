Bottom line

A slow launch keeps the application CPUs just as clocked and just as busy, but they retire fewer instructions. Per token, it consumes more cycles while retiring fewer instructions and moving less DRAM data. The missing time is therefore not ordinary extra work. It is consistent with longer residency in an instruction-light, on-CPU wait state.
